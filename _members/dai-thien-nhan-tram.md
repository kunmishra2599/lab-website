### Brief
Nhan completed his PhD at the Department of Pharmacy, National University of Singapore. His thesis focused on the development of bacteria-trapping nanonets as innovative anti-infectious biomaterials.  He recently joined the lab to work on a collaborative project, where he will investigate the link between lophenol, and intermediate product of the cholesterol biosynthetic pathway, and aging.
